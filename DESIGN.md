# Design System

## Design Goals

- Simple
- Clean
- Mobile-friendly
- Easy to understand
- Farmer-friendly

## Farmer Screens

1. Login
2. Dashboard
3. Add Produce
4. My Listings
5. Recommended Price
6. Buyer Requests
7. Orders
8. Delivery Tracking

## Buyer Screens

1. Login
2. Dashboard
3. Search
4. Product Details
5. Place Order
6. Orders
7. Tracking

## Components

- Navbar
- Sidebar
- Cards
- Forms
- Tables
- Charts
- Buttons
- Alerts

## Dashboard

Farmer:

- Current recommended price
- Produce listings
- Orders
- Buyer requests

Buyer:

- Available produce
- Price comparison
- Orders
- Delivery

## Design Rule

Do not introduce a completely different visual style
for every page.
