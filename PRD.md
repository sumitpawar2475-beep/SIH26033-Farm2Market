# SIH26033 - Farm2Market

## 1. Product Overview

Farm2Market is an AI-powered digital platform that connects
farmers directly with buyers and helps reduce unnecessary
intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

## 2. Problem Statement

Farmers may receive lower prices because multiple
intermediaries participate between producers and buyers.

Consumers/buyers may pay higher prices because of multiple
margins and inefficient transportation.

## 3. Objectives

- Direct farmer-buyer connection
- Better price transparency
- AI-based price recommendation
- Demand forecasting
- Farmer-buyer matching
- Logistics optimization
- Order management
- Supply-chain transparency

## 4. Users

### Farmer

- Register
- Add produce
- View recommended price
- Receive buyer requests
- Accept orders
- Track delivery

### Buyer

- Register
- Search produce
- Compare prices
- Place orders
- Track orders

### Administrator

- Manage users
- Monitor transactions
- View analytics

## 5. MVP

The MVP must support:

Farmer
→ Add Produce
→ AI Price Recommendation
→ Buyer Search
→ Buyer Order
→ Matching
→ Logistics
→ Delivery
→ Dashboard

## 6. AI Features

### Price Prediction

Inputs:
- Crop
- Location
- Historical price
- Season
- Quantity
- Demand

Output:
- Predicted price
- Recommended range

### Demand Forecasting

Predict expected demand.

### Matching

Match farmers and buyers based on:
- Crop
- Quantity
- Location
- Price
- Delivery date

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

- Secure authentication
- Responsive UI
- Maintainable code
- Reliable database
- API-based architecture
- Scalable design

## 8. Success Criteria

A complete end-to-end demonstration should work.
